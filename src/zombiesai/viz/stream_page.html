The agent, live

A reinforcement-learning agent learning Nacht der Untoten the way a person would: it sees the screen, presses keys and moves the mouse, and nothing else. Its policy started as a copy of human play and is being fine-tuned with PPO, several games at once. The stream shows one of them; the numbers are over every game the run has played.
